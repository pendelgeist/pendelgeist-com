What this is

A data-driven look at Nasubi's 11-month ordeal on Susunu! Denpa Shonen (1998-99) and the Korea sequel that followed — every postcard, prize, and dead end, transcribed and translated from the original broadcast.

How to read it

Scroll top to bottom for the curated analysis: numbers at a glance, monthly trajectory, best/worst wins, category breakdowns, and running commentary. Table headers are clickable to sort. Jump to Browse Data at the bottom to search the full raw dataset yourself.

A note on the numbers

JPY values are as declared/estimated at the time. A few entries in the source data are illegible or ambiguous (marked "?" or "probably") and are left as-is rather than guessed at.

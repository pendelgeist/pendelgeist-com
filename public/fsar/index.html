What This Is

Long-form reviews of shows I actually watched all the way through. If something was garbage I stopped early and it never got one of these — that's what Very Quick Anime Reviews is for, where every new show gets five to ten minutes and a one-liner.

What's In One

Overall story • overall production • the opening • the ending • anything else of particular note • spoilers, kept separate

Spoilers

Spoilers live in their own collapsed section at the bottom of every review and stay shut until you open them, so a link to a review is safe to follow before you've watched the show.

Not Just New Shows

Plenty of these are decades old. Use the Decade filter, and check the availability note on a review before assuming you can stream it.

In Progress

A review tagged In Progress is a draft I'm still revising. It'll change. Once it's settled it loses the badge.

What this is

A fun data-driven look at everything logged in Very Quick Anime Reviews — every rating, every season, crunched into stats. Separate page, freer format, no build step: this is all computed live from the same review data VQAR itself reads.

How to read it

Numbers at a glance up top, then a handful of curated angles on the data (rating distribution, ratings over time, best/worst rated, how "Finish Ep" shows held up on a full re-review, OP/ED favorites, most-used words). Jump to Browse Data at the bottom to search every review directly.

A note on the numbers

Only entries with a numeric rating count toward averages/rankings - a review can have just a ratingText and still be perfectly valid. Data is fetched fresh on every visit, so this page is only ever as current as the underlying VQAR gists.

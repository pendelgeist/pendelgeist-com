What's a Fact vs. a Theory?

Facts are things stated or directly shown on-screen (dates, character roles, terminology).

Theories are popular fan readings inferred from the text — reasonable, but not confirmed outright.

Unknowables are questions the series poses and pointedly never answers, on purpose.

Spoilers throughout, for the original TV series and The End of Evangelion.
